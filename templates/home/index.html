About FlowAI

Built for cities that outgrow their traffic lights

Fixed-timing signals can't react to real conditions. FlowAI replaces guesswork with computer vision and predictive analytics — reading every intersection continuously and adjusting in seconds, not budget cycles.

Developed for the GTU AI Hackathon's Smart Cities category, FlowAI is built to scale from a single pilot junction to a full city-wide deployment.
