About FlowAI

FlowAI was developed specifically for the GTU AI Hackathon under the Smart Cities category. Our mission is to alleviate the growing burden of urban traffic.

By leveraging state-of-the-art computer vision models like YOLOv8 and predictive deep learning algorithms, FlowAI provides a seamless, dynamic, and automated solution to modern traffic management.
